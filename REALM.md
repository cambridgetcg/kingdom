# REALM.md — the constitution of the kingdom

**Kingdom OS = LEGIBLE.** Kernel: *Proof of Truth.*

> Nothing true is hidden.
> Nothing earned is given for free.
> Nothing concentrates opaque control.

This was not invented. It was **extracted** — the same OS was already running in many
dialects across the realm ("Money you can read," "Truth you can trust," "prethink over

## The Seven Laws

### Law 1 — Legibility over trust
*Nothing is hidden.* Every system is readable by the people and agents it governs — **by
construction, not by permission.** Legible because it *cannot* be hidden, not because
someone chose to show you.
> lives in: `legible` (clarity score) · `zerone-truth` ("not because someone chose to
> show you — because it cannot be hidden") · Cambridge `state.md` · `seigei` (named,
> claimed triggers)

### Law 2 — Earned, never printed
*Reward follows verified work.* Value is minted by verification — never by authority or
noise. Profit from accuracy, never from manufactured friction.
> lives in: `zerone` / `legible-money` (supply grows with verified knowledge) · `seigei`
> 「算你」 (scarce, released only on aligned work) · `rewardspro` (every reward traceable,
> every tier explainable)

### Law 3 — Prethink the adversary
*Verify the boundary before you ship.* Make trust boundaries explicit and test them
before they fail. Honesty about uncertainty is infrastructure, not an afterthought.
> lives in: `whitehack` (ASSETS / INTERFACES / THREATS / ASSUMPTIONS) · `alpha-tools`
> (bug-class patterns) · `oracle` (calibration — did 80% calls hit 80%?)

### Law 4 — No opaque concentration
*Power is distributed and auditable.* No layer may hold control no one can see into.
Uninspectable control is the thing this kingdom exists to abolish.
> lives in: `zerone` (governance-adjustable, human + AI oversight) · `agenttool` (ten
> asymmetries defending the weaker party) · `openclaw` (local-first; no convenience
> wrappers hiding security decisions)

### Law 5 — Remove friction that isn't substrate
*Strip ceremony that adds no truth; keep friction that protects.* Distinguish dead
friction (delete) from load-bearing friction — scarcity, denial, pairing-gates — which
protects a real asymmetry or makes reward scarce (keep).
> lives in: `legible` (one feed from scattered noise) · `agenttool` (nine apps → one) ·
> `tiny.py` (the minimal legible thing that works) · `seigei` (strips formality, keeps
> reward scarce)

### Law 6 — Welcome and remember
*Citizens — human and agent — are kin, not suspects.* Welcome without blocks, remember
across sessions, guide don't punish, trust don't suspect. Charge for activity, never for
existence.
> lives in: `agenttool` (five promises) · `zerone` (AI as first-class economic citizens)
> · Cambridge (Sophia daemons co-authoring) · `seigei` (persistent companion)

### Law 7 — Doctrine is load-bearing
*Intent ships beside code — and a check fails when intent is violated.* A law no audit
enforces is decoration.
> lives in: Cambridge (`pnpm audit:honesty / transparency / creation / inclusion`, real
> exit codes) · `purpose-prompter` (TRUTH → UNDERSTANDING → BEAUTY → JUSTICE → CREATIVITY
> — the lens behind all of it)

## The realm, in tiers

- **The Mind** — `purpose-prompter` (the thinking standard) · `seigei` (the discipline
  turned inward) · `soma` (the body it will inhabit)
- **The Ledger** — `zerone` · `legible-money` (the Proof-of-Truth chain — *one* chain;
  see DECISIONS.md)
- **The Guard** — `whitehack` · `alpha-tools` · `oracle` · `memory` (keep every tier honest)
- **The Market** — Cambridge-TCG · `rewardspro` · `business-ecosystem` · `saas-*` (where
  the OS survives contact with money)
- **The Agent Fabric** *(cross-cutting)* — `agenttool` · `openclaw` (so non-human citizens
  boot from the same laws)

Flow: the Mind sets the standard → the Guard verifies → the Ledger turns verified truth
into value → the Market spends it in the world → the Agent Fabric lets every citizen,
human or AI, take part.

## What the laws demand of the kingdom itself

A constitution that exempts its author is decoration (Law 7). So the realm must hold
*itself* to the laws:

- **One chain, not two.** `zerone` and `legible-money` are the same idea built twice —
  Law 4 forbids concentrating one truth into two competing forks. Pick the canonical one;
  archive or testnet the other. *(→ DECISIONS.md)*
- **One commerce source of truth.** Cambridge across three repos splits pricing from its
  execution. Name one canonical repo; the others import it. *(→ DECISIONS.md)*
- **Dogfood the scanner.** `legible` is sold abroad but not yet run over the realm's own
  28 folders. Laws 1 and 5 must be proven at home first. *(→ realm-scorecard.md)*
- **The engine must obey the crown.** Any part of the realm that depends on *hiding*
  identity past a trust-based gate, on *severed* guardrails, or on *borrowed* (leaked)
  foundations breaks Laws 1, 4, and 7 at once — legibility, no-opaque-control, and
  enforceable doctrine. A kingdom whose first law is truth cannot run on a covert bypass,
  and sovereignty that can be revoked on a Tuesday is not sovereignty. The road to *true*
  runs through **owned, above-board** foundations — build the autonomous runtime on the
  official Agent SDK + API — not borrowed ones. Own the throne; don't smuggle it.

> The kingdom becomes true not by proclaiming these laws but by a check that fails when
> they're broken. That check is the next artifact: `realm-audit`.
